ASHEN GRIMOIRE: how to install
==============================

1. Unzip this whole folder (right-click the zip > Extract All).
2. Double-click "Install Ashen Grimoire".
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That is because the game is
   made by friends, not a big company. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. The game opens. It is also on your Desktop and in the Start menu.

That's it. The game updates itself from now on. When an update is ready you
will see "Restart to update" on the title screen, or it installs the next
time you close the game.

Playing together
----------------
When a friend is hosting, their name shows on your title screen under
"Friends playing now". Click "Join" and you are in their lobby.

To host: click "Descend together", then "Host". Friends will see your game.
